P1S Pen Plotter

Bed 256 x 256 mm, grid every 10 mm, origin front-left. Shaded edges: the pen cannot reach there.

Editing: drag an SVG to move it. Corner handles scale (Shift = stretch freely), edge handles stretch one way, the round knob rotates (Shift = 15° steps); hold Alt to scale from the centre. Arrow keys nudge 1 mm (Shift = 10 mm), Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates. Scroll over an SVG to scale it.

Pick which pen draws each colour. Give two colours the same pen number to draw them with one pen, or choose Skip to leave a colour out. Between pens the printer lifts, comes to the front and pauses so you can swap the pen.

In Bambu Studio: untick Bed Leveling, Flow Dynamics Calibration and Timelapse. Do not fit the pen until the printer pauses at the front after homing. At each pause: set the pen tip on the 10 mm cube, tighten, remove the cube, then Resume.

Offset = where the pen tip is relative to the nozzle tip (+X right, +Y back). Use the Calibrate tab to measure it.

Holder reach = how far the front of the pen holder sticks out in front of the nozzle. The nozzle then always stays at least that much + 5 mm back from the front edge, and the pen is fitted and changed there instead of at Y20. The shaded strip at the front of the bed shows where the pen cannot go.

Setting the pen with the cube: at the start and at every pen change the head waits at the front with the nozzle at Z16. Stand the 10 mm cube on the bed under the pen, slide the pen down until its tip rests on the cube, tighten it, then remove the cube before pressing Resume. The pen tip is then exactly 6 mm below the nozzle. Z heights are where the nozzle goes; the pen tip is 6 mm lower.

Pause 0 uses M400 U1: the printer waits until you press Resume on the screen or in the app. A number of seconds gives you that long to clip the pen on instead.

First run? Do a dry run with Pen down Z raised by 5 mm so the pen draws in the air.

  1. Download the calibration job and print it (pen not fitted at the start).
  2. After homing it pauses at the front with the nozzle at Z16: stand the 10 mm cube under the pen, rest the pen tip on it, tighten, remove the cube, then press Resume.
  3. It draws a 30 mm cross and a 10 mm circle where it thinks the pen is at bed centre, then parks the nozzle over bed centre and pauses again.
  4. Measure from the nozzle tip to the cross centre: X error = cross X - nozzle X (cross to the right = positive), Y error = cross Y - nozzle Y (cross towards the back = positive).
  5. Enter the errors below and press Apply: they are ADDED to the current offsets. Repeat until the error is under ~0.5 mm.
