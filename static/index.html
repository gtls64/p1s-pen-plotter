Mouse and keyboard

Drag to move. Corner handles scale (Shift: free), edge handles stretch, the round knob rotates (Shift: 15° steps), Alt scales from the centre. Scroll to scale. Arrows nudge 1 mm (Shift: 10 mm), Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates, Esc deselects.

Pens & fills

How pens and fills work

Each colour gets a pen. Give two colours the same number to draw them together, or Skip one. Between pens the head comes to the front and pauses for the swap.

Solid draws lines one pen width apart. Hatch uses your gap and angle; Cross-hatch adds a second set at 90°.

Printing it

In Bambu Studio, untick Bed Leveling, Flow Dynamics Calibration and Timelapse. Fit the pen only when the head pauses at the front after homing. At each pause, rest the pen tip on the 10 mm cube, tighten, take the cube away and press Resume.

Pen position

Offset is the pen tip relative to the nozzle (+X right, +Y back); measure it on the Calibrate tab. Holder reach is how far the holder sticks out in front of the nozzle.

Heights

Setting the pen with the cube

At the start and at every pen change the head waits at the front with the nozzle at Z16. Stand the 10 mm cube under the pen, slide the pen down onto it, tighten, then remove the cube and press Resume. The tip is then 6 mm below the nozzle. Z values are nozzle heights.

First run: raise Pen down Z by 5 mm so the pen draws in the air.

Motion

Pause 0 waits until you press Resume. A number of seconds continues on its own after that long.

Draw the target

  1. Print the calibration job, pen out.
  2. At the first pause, set the pen on the 10 mm cube, remove the cube, Resume.
  3. It draws a 30 mm cross and 10 mm circle where it thinks the pen is at bed centre, then parks the nozzle over bed centre and pauses.

Measure & correct

Error = cross position minus nozzle position (cross to the right or further back is positive). It is added to the current offsets; repeat until under 0.5 mm.
